# Themes

Written from commit `4682fe5`.

## What it is

A theme is colours, sizes and shapes for every part of the window, plus
what an installed package adds: pictures, sounds, layers, motion, a
`layout.json` of its own. The kit draws with it; the app chooses it.

- The kit's `Theme` (`crates/yapfast-iced-kit/src/theme.rs:447`, its fields
  generated by the macro there) carries the palette, metrics, type styles
  and spacing. `Theme::worked_out` (`theme.rs:955`) derives what the view
  reads directly (each part's colour over its ground, outlines, text
  styles); it's called whenever the theme changes (`app/mod.rs:246`).
- **Named parts**: every themed element has a name from one registry,
  `yapfast_core::themes::parts::REGISTRY`
  (`crates/yapfast-core/src/themes/parts.rs:177`), each entry a `PartInfo`
  (`parts.rs:140`) with the states it takes. A theme's `parts` layer is
  resolved once at load into a `PartTable`, and looked up per draw with
  `Theme::part` (`theme.rs:824`) into a `PartLook`
  (`crates/yapfast-iced-kit/src/parts.rs:61`: ground, ink, border, radius,
  shadow, opacity, padding, weight, case, italic, picture, hidden, and
  what styled it — `from_base`, `from_states`).
- Widgets apply it through `parts::Dress` / `parts::show`
  (`crates/yapfast-iced-kit/src/parts.rs:400`), which wrap the part's
  content in the theme's ground, picture and uneven sides. With the
  inspector off and nothing collecting, a part costs one thread-local read
  and no wrapper.
- Installed packages: `yapfast_core::themes::install`
  (`crates/yapfast-core/src/themes/install.rs:1`), checked by
  `themes/check.rs:1` (a maker's folder or a marketplace listing), with
  `layout.rs:1`, `lists.rs:1`, `motion.rs:1`, `media.rs:1` and
  `package.rs:1` for what a package may say. The window's side is
  `crates/yapfast-iced/src/themes.rs:1` (`Themes`), `theme_art.rs:1`
  (layers and art), `theme_chrome.rs:1`, `theme_effects.rs:1`,
  `theme_transition.rs:1` (changing channels under a package) and
  `theme_remote.rs:1` (the marketplace).

## Where state lives

| State | Home |
|---|---|
| The theme shown | `App::theme` (`app/mod.rs:201`), a `worked_out` `Theme` |
| Every theme the data folder offers | `App::themes` (`app/mod.rs:202`) |
| Which one `layout.json` chose | `layout.json`, through `crate::layout::Layout` (`layout.rs:1`) |
| `--theme-dev FOLDER`'s folder | `Themes::dev_folder` (`themes.rs:1`) |
| The inspector's on/off | `yapfast_iced_kit::inspect` (`inspect.rs:1`) |

## Flows

- **A theme is chosen** (Appearance, `themes ID`, or `layout.json` at
  start): `Themes` loads and resolves it, `App::theme` becomes its
  `worked_out` form, the app rebuilds its view; every part then reads
  `Theme::part`.
- **A part's look is added**: add its name and states to `REGISTRY`
  (`crates/yapfast-core/src/themes/parts.rs:177`), then draw it through
  `Dress`/`show` at the widget, and name it in `docs/themes.md`.
  `yapfast theme parts` prints the list, and `parts::docs_table` (`:987`)
  makes the doc's table, which a test keeps to the registry.
- **`--theme-dev`**: `themes::develop` (`lib.rs:236`) watches a folder and
  shows it over the chosen theme; Ctrl+Shift+T turns it off for the run
  (`app/layers.rs:466`); Ctrl+Shift+I turns the parts inspector on
  (`app/layers.rs:476`), which outlines the part under the pointer with its
  name, states and rule, and copies its name on a click
  (`crates/yapfast-iced-kit/src/inspect.rs:1`).

## Gotchas

- The registry is the only list of names; a part name not in it is
  accepted by the loader and does nothing (`docs/themes.md:394`).
- Parts are resolved once at load, pictures decoded then; the window only
  looks one up as it draws. Changing a theme mid-run means a new table.
- `worked_out` is not free (a whole table); it runs when the theme
  changes, not per frame.
- The inspector's tag is only added when the inspector is on **or** the
  test display is collecting parts for `dump` (`crates/yapfast-iced-kit/src/parts.rs:1`).
  Off both, no wrapper exists, so nothing can find a part at runtime.
- `--theme-dev`'s folder, not `layout.json`, wins: a save in it is shown
  while the watcher runs.
